# The `rtx2080` branch: this fork on an 8 GB Turing card

This branch is fxceless's fork with the patches that make the title run on an
NVIDIA RTX 2080 (8 GB, Turing, 32-lane waves). It is a work in progress for
people who want to try it; expect a few frames per second and visual bugs. You
need your own copy of the title; nothing of the title is in this repository.

## Build

```sh
cargo build --release -p victory
target\release\victory.exe run <game dir>\<the title's exe> --execute
```

See the README below for the paths `victory config` checks. A build with
`--profile diag --features diagnostics` logs the frame rate, card memory and
the switches below once a second.

## Settings these runs use

Set these in the environment before starting:

```
VICTORY_ALIAS_TARGETS=1
VICTORY_HEAP_SYNC_TABLES=1
VICTORY_LEVELS_ON_DEMAND=1
VICTORY_SHADER_OPT=0
VICTORY_WAVE_DEEP_SKIPS_NOT=922e1d0ee1e19a85,d7db57731ada919b,ab5455fc6abb804f,be6ce5d849d51127,191ecaed66eea3b1,31d703207b51549a,56f3bf2d9388e5a7,804b342cb4b9d1e9,abd7a22106898c66,1d44a723bdebbf27,b4b0427232707598,240cb516c1af0bc3,466ec41d3f9d24c0,65de113e23fa4fdd
```

- `VICTORY_ALIAS_TARGETS=1`: the title's render targets share video memory the
  way they share their heaps on the console (about 475 MB less card memory).
- `VICTORY_HEAP_SYNC_TABLES=1`: needed on this stack; without it, square
  see-through patches appear.
- `VICTORY_LEVELS_ON_DEMAND=1`: texture levels are made only when used.
- `VICTORY_SHADER_OPT=0` and the hash list: from Xeltax's VIcky notes; without
  them walls, ceilings and room light drop out.

## What to expect (2026-11-25, one RTX 2080 8 GB, Windows 11)

- The prologue plays from the first scene through the corridors, the common
  room and into the first aiming section, at 2-3 fps.
- Working: characters with textured clothes, hair and heads, walls, the TV
  picture.
- Known problems, most important first:
  - Card memory goes over an 8 GB card's budget (about 7.0 of 6.8 GB), so
    textures load late and things get worse the longer a session runs.
  - Indoors with haze (the common room, the bank) the picture washes out to
    white over time, the haze pulses, and moving people leave ghost copies.
    Under investigation.
  - Parts of some walls miss their colour (ragged dark or lit patches).
  - Faces and anything out of direct sun are very dark: the title's
    ray-traced bounce light needs its structure builder, which is off.

## Where the patches come from

On top of fxceless/victory-fork: LukeFarch's `nvidia-fx` branch, Xeltax's
VIcky heap map and wave64 switches, a port of VIctory #2226 (shared target
memory), and our own fixes for 64-lane shaders on 32-lane cards (paired lanes,
the f64 lane count, buffer record counts), the memory guards and texture
slots on the heap map. Commit messages name each change; all GPL-3.0.

Reports are welcome as issues on this fork: say your card, its memory, the
driver, and what you saw. Please do not post pictures or files of the title.
